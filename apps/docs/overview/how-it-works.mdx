<Steps>
  <Step title="A creator lists a prompt">
    A creator signs in with a Cardano wallet and lists a design prompt with a price. Simpuru stores the prompt and publishes its **SHA-256 hash** with the listing. The prompt itself stays hidden until someone pays. See [Sell a prompt](/guides/sell).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The buyer picks a prompt and pays">
    A person on the website, or an agent over MCP, picks a prompt and gets its price. They pay **instantly** (straight to the creator) or **with protection** (into the escrow). The escrow's input hash is bound to this listing and its content hash. See [Buy a prompt](/guides/buy).
  </Step>
  <Step title="The content is delivered and checked">
    The buyer gets the prompt right after paying. Simpuru posts a result hash on chain for the creator, and the buyer's side recomputes the hashes from what was delivered and compares them with the commitment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The watcher acts if something is wrong">
    No result by the deadline: the watcher **refunds** the buyer. A result that does not match: the watcher opens a **dispute**. Nobody has to notice. See [Protection watcher](/learn/protection-watcher).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Someone gets paid">
    If everything checks out, Simpuru collects from the escrow after the unlock time and pays the creator their share. If there was a dispute, the **arbiter** decides from on-chain evidence and pays the buyer or the creator. See [Disputes and the arbiter](/learn/disputes-and-the-arbiter).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Who does what

| Part | Done by |
| - | - |
| Locking, releasing and refunding money | The `vested_pay` escrow validator on Cardano |
| Prices, quotes and taking payments | The Simpuru API's x402 paywall, with its own facilitator |
| Posting results and collecting payment | Simpuru's seller agent, inside the API, on the creator's behalf |
| Paying creators their share | Simpuru's seller agent, after the escrow releases |
| Refunds and disputes for buyers | The protection watcher |
| Settling disputes | The arbiter, signing with Simpuru's arbiter key |

The full picture is in [Architecture](/developers/architecture) and the [Trust model](/learn/trust-model).
