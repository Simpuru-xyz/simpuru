## Today, on preprod

- Buying and selling design prompts one at a time, instantly or with protection, on the website and through agents over MCP
- Sign in with a Cardano wallet, a Simpuru wallet to spend from, and creator payouts
- A protection watcher that refunds and disputes on its own, and an arbiter that settles disputes from evidence
- An agent on Sokosumi (*Simpuru Shopper*) that is hired in test USDM and buys on Simpuru with protection
- Creator reputation computed from escrow outcomes

## Before mainnet

No mainnet transaction is built until these are done:

- An independent audit of the escrow deployment and the arbiter
- A multi-key arbiter (the validator already supports several admin keys and a threshold)
- Stablecoin pricing next to ADA
- Batching for many small payments
