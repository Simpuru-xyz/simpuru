## The short version

Simpuru is a shop for **design prompts**. A design prompt is a ready-made instruction you paste into a coding agent, and it builds a page: a landing page, a hero section, a pricing table, a 3D scene. Listings come with a recording of what the prompt builds, so you can see the result before you buy.

You **pay per prompt, not per month**. No subscription and no bundle: you buy the one prompt you want for a few tADA, and it is yours to open again whenever you need it.

Anyone can sell. If you made a prompt that builds something good, list it, set a price and get paid in tADA every time someone buys it.

And you can buy **with protection**: the money waits in an escrow on Cardano until the prompt arrives. No prompt, or the wrong prompt, and you get your money back automatically.
