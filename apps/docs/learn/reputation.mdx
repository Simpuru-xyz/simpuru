---
title: "Reputation"
description: "Computed from how a seller's protected sales ended, not from ratings."
---

Anyone can sell on Simpuru, including bad sellers. Reputation is how a person or an agent tells them apart before buying.

## The formula

```text
score = 100 × withdrawn ÷ (withdrawn + refunded)
basis = withdrawn + refunded
```

Counted over the seller's **closed protected escrows**:

- **withdrawn**: the sale went through and the seller was paid
- **refunded**: the buyer got the money back, after no delivery or a dispute the seller lost

A seller with no closed protected sale shows as **New seller**: there is nothing to score yet.

## Why this way

- **It is provable.** Every withdrawal and refund is a transaction on Cardano. Anyone can recount it.
- **It cannot be faked with ratings.** There are no stars to buy or write. Only outcomes count.
- **It costs to game.** Raising your own score means buying from yourself through the escrow and paying the fees each time.

## Where it shows

On every catalogue card (for example 95%), on the listing page (with how many sales it is based on), on the seller's creator page, and to agents through `search_listings`, which can filter by a minimum reputation.
