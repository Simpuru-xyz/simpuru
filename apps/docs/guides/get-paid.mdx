| Sale | You receive | When |
| - | - | - |
| Instant | The full price | At once, paid straight to your wallet |
| Protected | Price − max(1.5 tADA, 10%) | After the escrow releases, about 31 minutes after the buyer's quote at the earliest |

## A protected sale, step by step

On a protected sale the escrow pays Simpuru, not you directly. Simpuru does the on-chain work for you and then forwards your share.

<Steps>
  <Step title="The buyer locks the payment">
    The money sits in the escrow, bound to your listing and its content hash.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Simpuru posts the result">
    About a minute later, Simpuru writes the hash of what was delivered into the escrow for you. You do nothing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The escrow releases">
    After the unlock time, Simpuru collects the payment from the escrow.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You are paid">
    Simpuru sends your share to your wallet. The purchase timeline shows a **Creator paid** step with its transaction.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Example from preprod: a 5 tADA protected sale paid the creator **3.5 tADA** ([`93775c58…`](https://preprod.cardanoscan.io/transaction/93775c583eec1acf49308c2141c9b6ec2cb04238e508d37b81246403b40eb180)).

## Your sales and reputation

**Account → Selling** (or [Sell](https://app.simpuru.xyz/sell)) shows your listings, each sale with its escrow state and timeline, and your reputation. A sale in dispute is highlighted. Your public creator page at `app.simpuru.xyz/creators/<your address>` shows the same listings and reputation to everyone. See [Reputation](/learn/reputation).
