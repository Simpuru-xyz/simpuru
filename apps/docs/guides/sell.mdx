## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    Open [Sell](https://app.simpuru.xyz/sell) and sign in. Your account is the seller: buyers pay your wallet, and there is no address to type.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe it">
    Give it a title and a public description of what the prompt builds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Paste the prompt">
    Paste the full prompt. Nobody sees it until they pay. When you list, Simpuru hashes it, and that hash is what buyers check the delivery against.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a preview (optional, recommended)">
    A link to a recording of what the prompt builds: an `https` URL ending in `.mp4`, `.webm`, `.webp` or `.gif`. It becomes your card in the catalogue. It is public, so show the result, not the prompt.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a category, modes and price">
    The category puts your listing in the catalogue filter. Offer **instant** (from 1 tADA), **protected** (from 5 tADA) or both. The form shows what you receive per sale.
  </Step>
  <Step title="List it">
    The listing appears in the catalogue with its committed content hash.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Use your own work

Only list prompts and preview recordings you made or have the right to sell. The preview is the first thing buyers see.
