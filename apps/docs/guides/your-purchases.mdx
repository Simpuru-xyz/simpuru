**Account → Purchases** lists every purchase with its mode, price, status and date:

- **Open** shows the prompt again, for free.
- **Timeline** opens the purchase's page with every on-chain step.
- Protected purchases show the deadline they are waiting on, for example *refund if no delivery by 14:03*.

## The timeline

<Frame caption="A protected purchase from start to finish">
  <img src="/images/screens/app-purchase-timeline.jpg" alt="A purchase timeline: locked in escrow, result submitted, seller paid, creator paid" />
</Frame>

Each step links its transaction on [Cardanoscan (preprod)](https://preprod.cardanoscan.io). While a purchase is still open, the page counts down to the next deadline and shows what happens then.

| Status | Meaning |
| - | - |
| Locked in escrow | Your payment is in the escrow |
| Result submitted | The hash of what was delivered is on chain |
| Refund requested | You asked for a refund before any result |
| Disputed by buyer | The delivery did not match; the arbiter will decide |
| Seller paid | The escrow released the payment to Simpuru, which pays the creator next |
| Creator paid | The creator received their share |
| Buyer refunded | The money came back to you |
| Paid | An instant purchase, paid straight to the creator |
