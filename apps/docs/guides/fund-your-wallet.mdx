Every account has a **Simpuru wallet**: a wallet Simpuru holds for you on preprod. You fund it with tADA, you buy from it on the website, your agents buy from it within your limits, and you can send everything back to your own wallet at any time.

<Frame caption="Account → Wallet">
  <img src="/images/screens/app-account-wallet.png" alt="The Simpuru wallet card with balance, address, QR code and buttons" />
</Frame>

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your account">
    Click your address at the top right, then **Account**. The **Wallet** tab shows the balance, the wallet's address and a QR code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get test ADA">
    Click **Get test ADA**. It copies the address and opens the [preprod faucet](https://docs.cardano.org/cardano-testnets/tools/faucet) in a new tab. Paste the address there and request funds. You can also send tADA from your own wallet to the same address.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for it to arrive">
    The balance refreshes every 20 seconds. When the funds land, a note like **+10 tADA arrived** appears.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  A protected purchase needs at least 5 tADA plus about 0.2 tADA for the network fee.
</Tip>
