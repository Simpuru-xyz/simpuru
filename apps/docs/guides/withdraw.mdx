---
title: "Withdraw"
description: "Send your Simpuru wallet back to the wallet you signed in with."
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account → Wallet">
    Click your address at the top right, then **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Withdraw to my wallet">
    Click **Withdraw to my wallet**. The dialog shows how much will be sent and to which address: always the wallet you signed in with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm and wait for the transaction. The dialog links it on Cardanoscan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Good to know

- **2 tADA stay behind** to cover the network fee and keep the change above Cardano's minimum.
- The button is available once the wallet holds more than 3 tADA.
- Withdrawals always go to your own wallet. Nobody, including your agents, can send them elsewhere.
