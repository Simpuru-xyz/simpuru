## Before you start

- A Cardano wallet extension, for example [Eternl](https://eternl.io) or [Lace](https://www.lace.io)
- The wallet switched to the **preprod** network

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    Go to [app.simpuru.xyz](https://app.simpuru.xyz) and click **Sign in** at the top right.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your wallet">
    The dialog lists every Cardano wallet found in your browser. Pick yours.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign the message">
    Your wallet asks you to sign a one-time message. It proves the wallet is yours; nothing is paid and nothing leaves your wallet.
  </Step>
  <Step title="You're in">
    The top right now shows your short address and your Simpuru wallet balance. Its menu has Account, Selling, Connect an agent and Sign out.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Frame caption="Signed in: your address, your balance and the menu">

## Good to know

- A session lasts **7 days**. Sign out from the menu at any time.
- The first sign-in creates your account and its [Simpuru wallet](/guides/fund-your-wallet).
- If your wallet is on another network, the app asks you to switch it to preprod.
- The **Testnet · preprod** badge in the top bar is always there to remind you that test ADA has no value.
