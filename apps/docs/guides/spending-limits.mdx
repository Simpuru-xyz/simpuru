---
title: "Spending limits"
description: "How much your agents may spend per purchase and per day."
---

Your agents pay from your Simpuru wallet, and never more than the limits on the [Agents](https://app.simpuru.xyz/agents) page.

| Limit | Default | Allowed range |
| - | - | - |
| Max per purchase | 10 tADA | 1–100 tADA |
| Daily budget | 30 tADA | At least the max per purchase, up to 500 tADA |

Click **Edit** on the limits card to change them. The card also shows how much your agents spent today.

## What the limits cover

- They apply to purchases your **agents** make over MCP.
- Purchases you make yourself on the website are not capped by them and do not count toward the daily budget.
- Protected purchases your agent makes are refunded automatically if the prompt never arrives, like your own.
